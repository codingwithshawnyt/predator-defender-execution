# References

Curated bibliography. Group by theme.

## Predatory trading and adversarial execution (analytical)

- Brunnermeier, M. K., & Pedersen, L. H. (2005). "Predatory Trading." *Journal of Finance*, 60(4), 1825-1863.
- Carlin, B. I., Lobo, M. S., & Viswanathan, S. (2007). "Episodic Liquidity Crises: Cooperative and Predatory Trading." *Journal of Finance*, 62(5), 2235-2274.
- Schoneborn, T., & Schied, A. (2009). "Liquidation in the Face of Adversity: Stealth vs. Sunshine Trading." Working paper.
- Carmona, R., & Yang, J. (2011). "Predatory Trading: A Game on Volatility and Liquidity." Working paper.
- Schied, A., & Zhang, T. (2017). "A State-Constrained Differential Game Arising in Optimal Portfolio Liquidation." *Mathematical Finance*, 27(3), 779-802.
- Schied, A., & Zhang, T. (2019). "A Market Impact Game under Transient Price Impact." *Mathematics of Operations Research*, 44(1), 102-121.
- Micheli, A., Muhle-Karbe, J., & Neuman, E. (2023). "Closed-Loop Nash Competition for Liquidity." *Mathematical Finance*, 33(4), 1082-1118.

## Optimal execution (single-agent)

- Almgren, R., & Chriss, N. (2000). "Optimal Execution of Portfolio Transactions." *Journal of Risk*, 3, 5-39.
- Bertsimas, D., & Lo, A. W. (1998). "Optimal Control of Execution Costs." *Journal of Financial Markets*, 1(1), 1-50.
- Cartea, A., Jaimungal, S., & Penalva, J. (2015). *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading*. Cambridge University Press.

## RL for execution

- Ning, B., Lin, F. H. T., & Jaimungal, S. (2021). "Double Deep Q-Learning for Optimal Execution." *Applied Mathematical Finance*, 28(4), 361-380.
- Macri, A., & Lillo, F. (2024). "Reinforcement Learning for Optimal Execution When Liquidity is Time-Varying." arXiv:2402.12049.
- Hafsi, Y., & Vittori, E. (2024). "Optimal Execution with Reinforcement Learning." arXiv:2411.06389.
- Capponi, A., Menkveld, A. J., & Zhang, H. (2024). "Reinforcement Learning for Continuous-Time Optimal Execution." *Review of Finance*.

## Multi-agent RL in markets (direct prior art)

- Lillo, F., & Macri, A. (2024, revised 2026). "Deviations from the Nash Equilibrium and Emergence of Tacit Collusion in a Two-Player Optimal Execution Game with Reinforcement Learning." arXiv:2408.11773. **Direct precedent we extend.**
- Cheridito, P., Dupret, J.-L., & Wu, Z. (2025). "ABIDES-MARL: A Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Environment for Endogenous Price Formation and Execution in a Limit Order Book." arXiv:2511.02016.
- Karpe, M., Fang, J., Ma, Z., & Wang, C. (2020). "Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning in a Realistic Limit Order Book Market Simulation." ICAIF '20.
- Cont, R., & Xiong, W. (2022). "Dynamics of Market Making Algorithms in Dealer Markets: Learning and Tacit Collusion." *Mathematical Finance*.

## Empirical game-theoretic analysis (EGTA)
